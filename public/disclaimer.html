No Guarantee of Housing or Services

Effective November 10, 2026

Please read this before you rely on a match, a screener result, or a referral.

Using this app, getting a match, seeing “likely” in the screener, or sending a Refer-me request does not guarantee housing, shelter, a bed tonight, rent or utility money, benefits, a job, or any other service. It also doesn’t guarantee that anyone will contact you by a certain time.

Why we can’t promise

  • Programs decide for themselves. Every program and agency sets its own eligibility rules, waitlists, hours and funding. Only they can say yes.
  • Openings change daily. Shelter beds, rent funds and energy assistance can run out quickly.
  • Our information can be out of date. We try to verify listings, but phone numbers, addresses, hours and rules change. Listings that staff haven’t recently checked are labeled “not yet verified”. Always call ahead.
  • The screener is an estimate. “Likely” and “maybe” are based on a few answers and general income limits (federal poverty guidelines we update yearly). The real decision uses rules and documents we don’t check.
  • Refer me is a request, not an enrollment. Staff will try to reach you, usually within a few business days, but we can’t promise a response time or that a program will accept you.

Not professional advice

Nothing in this app is legal, medical, mental health, financial, tax or immigration advice. For advice about your situation, talk to a qualified professional. Free options include Oregon Law Help (oregonlawhelp.org), the New Avenues Legal Clinic, or CASH Oregon for taxes.

Not an emergency service

No one is watching this app around the clock. If you are in danger, call 911. For support any time, call or text 988. Ages 9–17 can go to the Alba Collaborative 24/7 center at 470 SE 165th Ave (971-754-4350). Call to Safety is available 24/7 at 503-235-5333.

Other organizations

Programs listed here, including New Avenues for Youth, government agencies and nonprofits, are independent. A listing doesn’t mean they endorse us or that we endorse them, unless a listing is marked “Partner”. We aren’t responsible for their services, decisions, websites or privacy practices.

Keep looking

Apply to more than one program when you can. If something doesn’t work out, use your check-in code to tell us what got in the way and refresh your matches. You can also call 211 or text your ZIP code to 898211.
