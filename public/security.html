Security

Young people trust us with things they may not tell anyone else. Protecting that information comes first, ahead of convenience.

How we protect information

  • We collect as little as possible. Names aren’t needed, and the screener saves nothing.
  • Connections are encrypted. Contact details and notes are also encrypted inside our database.
  • Every staff member has their own account with a strong password. Admin accounts require two-step sign-in.
  • Accounts lock after repeated failed sign-ins, and sessions end after 30 minutes of inactivity.
  • Staff see only what their role needs. Every record they open is logged.
  • Check-in codes can’t be guessed: repeated wrong codes are blocked.
  • The site blocks cross-site attacks, framing and injected scripts.
  • Old records are deleted on a schedule. Anyone can delete their own record with their code.

Found a security problem? Tell us.

If you’re good with computers and think you’ve found a weakness, we want to hear from you. Email ripchoom@gmail.com with the subject “Security report”. Tell us what you found and how to reproduce it.

Good-faith rules. We won’t pursue legal action against you for research that follows all of these:

  • Never view, copy, change or delete anyone else’s information. If you come across someone’s data by accident, stop, don’t keep it, and tell us.
  • Use only your own test searches and your own check-in codes.
  • Don’t disrupt the service. No denial-of-service attacks, spam, or flooding Refer-me with requests.
  • Don’t try to trick staff (social engineering) and don’t attack staff accounts.
  • Give us reasonable time to fix the problem before telling anyone else.

We’ll reply and keep you updated, and with your permission we’ll thank you publicly. Anything outside these rules may be treated as unauthorized access (see our Terms of Use).

Staff: if something seems wrong

Lost your phone or laptop? Got a sign-in alert you don’t recognize? Did someone ask you for a password or check-in code? Tell a main architect or maintenance staff member right away. They can sign you out everywhere and reset your account. We will never ask for your password, by email, text or phone.
